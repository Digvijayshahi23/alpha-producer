# AlphaForge Roadmap

## V1: Foundation (Current)
* Stable local application architecture
* Alpha Workbench (UI for authoring expressions)
* Alpha Library (Save, tag, and organize alphas)
* Alpha Descriptions (Structured explanations of idea, rationale, etc.)
* Research Notes (Markdown notes attached to alphas)
* Data/Operator Explorer (Searchable catalogs of fields and operators)
* Experiment Tracking (Capture hypotheses, parameters, and results)
* Clean Dashboard (Overview of research activity)

## V2: Advanced Analytics
* Advanced alpha generation assistance
* Alpha templates (Save and reuse common structural patterns)
* Batch experimentation (Run variations across parameters)
* Better simulation analysis (Visualize performance deeply)
* Alpha comparison (Side-by-side metric comparisons)
* Correlation analysis (Identify orthogonal alphas)
* Research session analytics (Summarize findings from sessions)

## V3: Intelligent Research Assistant
* Intelligent alpha assistant
* LLM-assisted alpha explanation
* Alpha mutation/generation
* Research recommendation system
* Automated experiment pipelines
* Advanced portfolio/factor analytics
